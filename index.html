The accessibility guide

Small changes. Big difference.

Visual accessibility

Help everyone see the important things.

Use readable text, strong color contrast, and layouts that do not rely on color alone.

  • Choose clear fonts and comfortable text sizes.
  • Add alternative text to meaningful images.
Read the guide

Hearing accessibility

Let people follow along without sound.

Captions and transcripts help people who are deaf or hard of hearing, and they help when audio cannot be played.

  • Provide accurate captions for spoken content.
  • Do not share important information through sound alone.
Read the guide

Physical accessibility

Make navigation work in different ways.

Some people use a keyboard, switch device, voice input, or other tools instead of a mouse.

  • Make every interactive element keyboard accessible.
  • Show a visible focus indicator.
Read the guide

Cognitive accessibility

Keep the experience calm and clear.

Simple language, consistent navigation, and helpful instructions make content easier for many people.

  • Use short sentences and descriptive headings.
  • Explain errors and how to fix them.
