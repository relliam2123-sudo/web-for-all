Why Web For All?

A more inclusive web starts with understanding.

Web For All is a student-created educational website that promotes digital accessibility and inclusion. We make accessibility easier to understand through simple explanations, practical examples, and learning activities.

Our vision

A digital world without barriers.

A world where people can access information and opportunities without unnecessary barriers, regardless of their abilities.

Our mission

Make accessibility easier to understand.

To raise awareness about digital accessibility and encourage thoughtful website design through easy-to-follow guides and learning activities.

Core values

  • Inclusivity — Making space for different needs and experiences.
  • Equality — Supporting fair access to online information.
  • Empathy — Considering the challenges others may face.
  • Responsibility — Making ethical choices when designing technology.
  • Continuous Learning — Staying open to better and more accessible solutions.

Professional ethics we applied

Accessibility & Inclusion

We follow WCAG 2.1 guidance: strong contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader friendly structure, and text-to-speech through the Web Speech API.

Data Privacy & Security

There is no backend, database, tracking, or network request. The contact form is a demo, and only your display settings are saved in your own browser.

Intellectual Property & Honesty

This site was built by our student team. We credit our sources and openly disclose that an AI assistant helped draft and polish parts of the design and code, which we reviewed.
